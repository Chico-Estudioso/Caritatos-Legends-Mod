[h1]Caritatopc — Brotato Legends Collection[/h1]
One mod that brings back classic Brotato mods ("legends"), balances them and gives each one its own interactive touch. Toggle and tune them from the in-game Caritatopc menu. No other mods required.
[list]
[*][b]Fish Hook[/b] (Abyssal Terrors): guaranteed Fish Hook in the shop of your chosen wave and custom curse chance. [i]"It bites!" fishing minigame: catch it for a free reroll.[/i]
[*][b]Eternal Harvest[/b]: no harvesting decay in endless (or keep it growing), Piggy Bank keeps working. [i]Golden harvest: reap the wheat at its peak for materials.[/i]
[*][b]Merchant[/b]: item and reroll prices (0% = free rerolls), optional no per-wave inflation. [i]Haggling: stop the needle in the green zone for a shop discount.[/i]
[*][b]Supermarket[/b] (very strong, off by default): order the item or weapon you want. You earn one order in each wave where you defeat an elite or a boss, kept until you spend it; orders cost 50% more and arrive locked in the next shop. [i]Open the order box by tapping.[/i]
[*][b]Gift[/b] (co-op): in the shop, press one of your weapons or items → [b]Gift[/b] (next to Recycle). The other player accepts it with their controller in "A gift from your bro!", sealed with a fist bump. Mechanized and cursed weapons can be gifted; the Tumor can't.
[*][b]Crates[/b]: at the end of a wave, fuse unopened crates into a legendary crate. The first fusion takes 3 crates, then 6 and 9. [i]The crates come together, flash and turn into a legendary one.[/i]
[*][b]Fusion[/b] (very strong, off by default): at the start of a run, fuse with another character and add its effects to yours, in exchange for less experience, higher prices and more enemies. [i]"Bro fusion!" with a fist bump.[/i]
[*][b]Mouse[/b]: the mouse shows up and works even with controllers. In co-op the cursor can go anywhere, but it only clicks and scrolls on the panel of the keyboard and mouse player.
[*][b]Tato Workshop[/b]: "Your tatos", a pixel editor to draw your potato's look (or import a PNG) and wear it in runs; and a power meter that warns when a character or item is too strong. To create characters and items you need [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3817655214]BroEditor[/url] (by Mojimoon): your tatos show up among its icons.
[/list]
